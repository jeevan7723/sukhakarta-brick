01

Clay Selection & Refining

We source fertile, alluvial clay and river silt with optimum silica-alumina balance. The raw earth is weathered for 30 days and mechanically screened to eradicate stones, gravel, vegetation, and harmful alkali salts.

Metric: < 0.2% organic matter • 0% stones
02

Precision Molding

Tempered clay pug is compacted into precision steel molds. Crisp 90° arris corners and a 12mm deep mortar frog indentation ("SUKHAKARTA") are embossed, creating superior mechanical shear interlock when laid on site.

Metric: ±2mm dimensional consistency
03

Natural Sun Drying

Raw "green" bricks are stacked in staggered open-air drying sheds for 7 to 10 days. Gradual natural moisture evaporation prevents micro-shrinkage fractures and eliminates internal stress prior to entering the kiln.

Metric: Moisture dropped safely to < 2%
04

Kiln Burning & Annealing

Fired in continuous Bull's Trench kilns at 1,000°C–1,080°C. Silicates melt and vitrify the entire brick body. Slowly cooled and annealed over 5 days, yielding maximum compressive strength, weather proofing, and metallic resonance.

Metric: Fired at 1,050°C • High load capacity
